## Sequential implementation steps

1. Load Q01's frozen suite census and perform mandatory preflight plus independent S0 collection under `suiteEntryId` before registering any attempt. Failed preflight or S0 remains setup-failed in the census, with no M1/M7 attempted sample; untouched entries remain unrun. On successful setup, register `evaluationAttemptId`, bind the S0 hash, and immediately dispatch the real graph. Failures after registration, including a crash before a result, stay attempted/stalled and cannot be moved retrospectively into setup. Ordinary resume/retry keeps the same identity.

2. Bind the actual R01 graph to stateful fake providers, then actual models with fake providers, then scoped live accounts under the existing separate modes. Persist model/prompt/policy/schema/evaluator/fixture/source release identities and the exact mode. Simulated events or canned model text cannot establish model behavior.

3. Use the S0 hash already bound at registration; capture original model outputs/failures, actual stage/span and tool attempts, authentic approval decisions, dispatch/readback, success claim IDs, and Q02 independent S1/history. Keep evidence collection outside the graph's self-report. Human edits, corrections, repairs, and visibility windows must follow the predeclared actor/fault schedule.

4. Execute S1, S2, safe block, and stale approval first, with named negative tests for wrong recipients, missing drafts, phantom Slack, and incomplete evidence. Use barriers for duplicate starts/crash points. A failed case stays in its cohort even when a separately versioned correction or repair succeeds.

5. Execute all 18 family baselines and the additional repetitions to reach the planned 42 baseline/repetition attempts, then declared variants/repair legs. Complete at least five live scenarios: golden, replay, stale approval, draft verification, and safe block. If B08 recovery is deferred, keep its expected successful-recovery baseline unrun; a safe stop cannot pass that recovery contract.

6. Save original source/output digests for independent Q05 review before showing corrections. Save the complete census and all attempted/passed/failed/pending/unverified/unrun records, not merely assessment rows. Empty or failed registration/import gets an explicit harness diagnostic and cannot silently reduce the census.

7. Test stable denominator across resume/replay, interrupted registered starts, pre-registration S0 failures excluded from M1/M7, immutable S0 hash binding and no retrospective boundary changes, unrun slots, exhausted budgets, missing required artifacts, absent human labels, and claim-time versus later-drift evidence. Hand Q05 immutable census plus observed attempts and reports with raw IDs, counts, versions and command receipts.

## Do not

- Do not present generated event streams as executed workflows, mix fault/human actions into worker authority, drop failed attempts, or count a repair as the original clean success.
- Do not parallelize live cases in a shared incident/mailbox with conflicting resets. Separate test namespaces before running independent cases concurrently.
