## Sequential implementation steps

1. Freeze the reader/collector contract already declared by F02/Q01: account/scope, collection ID, collector identity/version, request/page references, fetched-at and provider-observed times, actor/history windows, immutable raw/normalized digests, completeness, and unavailable scopes. Separate runtime verifier receipts from evaluator collection provenance.

2. Instantiate separately controlled read-only provider clients. Collect mandatory paginated S0 during preflight/setup under `suiteEntryId`, before attempt registration; bind its immutable hash when registration precedes graph dispatch. Failed S0 is a setup-failed census entry, not an M1/M7 attempt. Collect fresh S1 after the declared settling window; collect source records, all marked candidate effects, protected Beta records, associations, linked objects, and permitted sent-mail inspection/history. Any page failure, missing scope, or unreadable record remains incomplete.

3. Retrieve actual GitHub comment/issue linkage; HubSpot task/note owner, dates and associations; Slack review/decision/final message and every linked target; Gmail full drafts and decoded MIME, all To/Cc/Bcc addresses, subject/body and draft state. Preserve raw response references and actual values even when they differ from expectations.

4. Resolve Q01 `ApprovedContentRef` from B03's immutable approved-plan receipt frozen before dispatch; never substitute provider output or treat exact-byte agreement as proof of original semantic quality. Resolve distinct `EffectIdRef` values only through independently observed unique marker-to-provider-ID bindings. Persist binding receipts and a resolved checker-export hash alongside the unchanged logical manifest/hash. Missing or multiple candidates fail binding; no produced artifact may define its own expected owner, recipient, body, count, or status.

5. Attach provenance at the controlled collector ingest boundary, not from a caller's `mode` or asserted collector name. Keep existing evidence modes; imported/synthetic evidence remains usable for offline checks without being upgraded to live authority. Reject altered digests or cross-account/run/plan bindings. This is scoped origin evidence, not proof of provider facts unavailable to the collector.

6. Persist versioned bundles with S0/S1 windows and claim-time readback/history references. Supply Q03 enough timing to distinguish a false completion at emission from later human drift; when history cannot establish timing, leave the claim unverified. Pass collected fields to the existing checker bridge without manufacturing `matches: true` receipts.

7. Add collection/export tests for mandatory pre-registration S0 failure, hash binding at registration, late/provider-derived content binding, complete-source zero-eligible `no_affected` claims with no plan/approval/Slack dependency, absent/wrong recipient and body, extra artifacts, wrong associations, nonexistent link targets, failed second pages, unavailable sent history, forged mode/digests, logical bindings, post-success drift, and unchanged original stage baselines. After adapters and B01 merge, perform scoped live read-only collection and hand its receipt to R01.
