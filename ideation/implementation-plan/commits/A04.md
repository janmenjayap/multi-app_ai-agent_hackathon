## Exact auditor invocation and authority boundary

Follow [agent/LLM integration](../07-agent-spawning-and-llm-integration.md).
Export `auditSemantics(input: AuditorInput, ctx: AgentInvocationContext, deps)`
from **auditor/index.ts**, returning `AgentCallResult<AuditVerdict>` through F02
schemas. R01's **src/server/workflow/nodes.ts** invokes it in `audit_semantics`
only after drafting and B03's deterministic proposal checks succeed.

**The actual request path is** graph node → `auditSemantics` → A01 `invokeRole`
→ A01 `dispatchStructured` → Gemini `models.generateContent`. **auditor/prompt.ts**
builds an independent context from original GitHub/HubSpot source projections,
all proposed customer-text entries, claim references and the supported task
contract. One logical invocation checks the whole fixed selected set; findings
identify their commitment and claim, with no omitted or extra draft scope.
**auditor/validate.ts** checks structured findings after raw-response persistence.
Exclude the analyst's narrative/rationale, drafter reasoning/confidence, shared
messages, prior verdict and desired answer; enforce exclusion in tests rather
than rely on the prompt to ignore leaked fields.

Use `role=auditor`, stable run/revision/role identity, and immutable source/draft
digests. A01 resolves optional `GEMINI_MODEL_AUDITOR` and enforces the same
recorded transport/schema budget. This role has no separate retry owner or model
client. Return findings and first-output references to R01; concerns route to
`safely_blocked`, required-stage exhaustion/refusal/unavailable output to `failed`
before protected effects, and a validated clear finding merely allows plan freeze.

Original sources come from R01's deterministic adapters; this role performs no
MCP/API app reads or writes. Slack approval belongs to B05/I04, protected execution
to B06, and fresh verification to B07 through the
[external-app boundary](../08-mcp-api-and-external-app-integration.md).
No auditor result may send a Slack approval, call a provider, repair a draft,
waive freshness, or become Q05's independent human label.

**Additional invocation acceptance:** inspect the exact A01 messages and verify
excluded content is absent; assert one logical call per valid role/revision,
zero model calls during ordinary approval resume, and preserved raw findings on
invalid output. R01 tests that blocked/failed audits never reach plan approval or
protected dispatch, including the enabled-auditor outage case.

## Auditor usefulness and measurement independence

Keep the preapproval auditor separate from the independent evaluation described in the [reliability plan](../06-agent-reliability-implementation.md).

1. Record the exact blind input projection and first auditor output via A01, including source/output digests and prompt/model/schema versions. Persist findings and failures before any correction/rerun.
2. Present findings as claim-specific concerns with original source references and reasons. A clean structured response only permits deterministic progression; it does not authenticate a source, approve a write or establish the final state.
3. Supply Q05 with both the proposed text and auditor verdicts for comparison against independently supplied human labels. Distinguish missed defects, correct detections and false blocks; do not score the auditor against its own generated labels.
4. Extend tests with a syntactically valid but unsupported citation, an omitted uncertainty statement, a correct draft that should not be blocked, leakage of drafter rationale/confidence and required-stage timeout/refusal.
5. Preserve enabled-stage failures in the canonical trace and prevent review progression after exhaustion. A successful later correction cannot erase the original auditor's missed defect or false block.

**Additional acceptance:** context-exclusion and deterministic routing tests pass; actual auditor usefulness remains unverified until human-labeled real outputs exist. Hand R01 concerns/failure routing and Q05 the raw comparison evidence without broadening auditor authority.

## Do not

- Do not feed the auditor another role's rationale/confidence, give it the desired answer, or let its verdict grant approval or override deterministic guards.
- Do not treat a clean audit as factual proof, a model-generated label as human ground truth, or a failed enabled auditor as optional during a run.
- Do not edit A03, A01, shared schemas, dependency files, storage, or graph composition on this branch.
